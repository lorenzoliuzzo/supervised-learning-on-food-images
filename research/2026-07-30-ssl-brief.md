# Research brief: Self-supervised pretraining for small CNNs on a single 8GB GPU: SimSiam, BYOL, DINO, masked autoencoders

**Caveat (added after review):** this list is unsynthesized and roughly a
third of it is off-topic noise from a mis-targeted retrieval (representation
theory of Lie/loop algebras, de novo drug/antibody design, quantum
architecture search) that happened to keyword-match "representation
learning." The load-bearing entries for this project are #17 (BYOL), #24
(SimSiam) and #28 (Barlow Twins) — the only sources that are actually
non-contrastive/negative-free SSL methods runnable at small batch on 8GB, the
constraint that already ruled out SimCLR (#1) in
`plans/2026-07-30-training-roadmap.md`. DINO and masked-autoencoder methods,
despite being named in the brief's own title, are entirely absent from the
retrieved sources — that gap is real and unaddressed here.
